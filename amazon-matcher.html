Amazon → Tiller Splitter

Splits each Amazon charge from your Tiller sheet into one line per product, with item details in MetaData and amounts that add up to the original charge. Everything is processed on this device only.

1 · Get your Amazon order data

  1. Open your Amazon orders page, signed in to the account you want.
  2. Click the Amazon Order History Reporter extension icon and pick the period to report (e.g. this year).
  3. Download two CSVs from the report: the orders view (matches each charge) and the items view (gives per-item prices for splitting).
  4. For a second account, sign in to it and repeat.
Open Amazon orders page

Backup option without the extension: Request Your Data from Amazon, choose Your Orders, and confirm the email. The download link arrives by email, from a few hours to a few days later; the .zip works here as-is and has everything needed for splitting.

2 · Load the Amazon files

Load the orders and items CSVs for each account. Select them all at once, or add them one at a time. Files with the same name are fine. The extension's transactions view is also accepted.

    3 · Paste your Tiller Amazon rows

    In Excel, filter or select your Amazon transactions and copy the full rows with the header row (all columns, including Amount and MetaData). Paste here.

    Copying a filtered view is fine. Rows that aren't Amazon charges are ignored.

    4 · Split lines

    Load the Amazon files and paste Tiller rows to see results.

    In Excel: delete the original transactions marked Split, then paste the new rows below your last transaction (they have the same columns as what you copied) and sort by date. Rows marked Not found aren't in the output, so keep those originals. Check means it matched, but glance at the split before you use it.
